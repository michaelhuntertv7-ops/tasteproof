Why this is different

Most cultural agents stop at recommendations. TasteProof treats cultural grounding as something that can be tested, regressed, and audited.

  • Repeatable cultural test scenarios
  • Qloo OFF vs Qloo ON framing
  • Evidence provenance separated from interpretation
  • Run-level SHA-256 digest in live mode
  • No fabricated fallback when Qloo fails

Safe-use boundary

Qloo results describe aggregate cultural affinities. TasteProof does not use them to infer identity, health, politics, finances, or eligibility, and does not claim they predict an individual's future behavior.

The evaluation harness uses explicit user-provided cultural anchors and bounded calls only.
