Why Qloo is necessary

Without Qloo, TasteProof can collect a proposal but cannot perform its central cultural-grounding test. Qloo creates the comparison set and evaluates the proposal against explicit taste signals.

  • Canonical workflows: recommend → rank
  • Proposal and alternatives ranked together
  • Evidence separated from interpretation
  • Digest-backed run identity

Responsible boundary

Qloo describes aggregate taste patterns. TasteProof does not claim to predict one person's behavior, infer sensitive identity, or make eligibility decisions.

Ambiguous or unresolved entities remain visible. Live failures never silently become fixture results.
